Who worked on a card, beside its time: one avatar alone, up to three stacked, and more than three as two avatars plus “+N”.

Taken from `.by-group`, `.by-av` and `.by-pop` in `Vellum-Lumen.html`. `Avatar` is a single seat.

- **Avatars:** a person is their initial on their own colour; a crew member is a neutral orb with its glyph (no crew hues). A small check on an avatar means *drafted by the crew, confirmed by you*.
- **At rest** the avatars overlap by 4px, each ringed in the card's colour (`--seal-ring`, the sticky's paper on a note). **On hover or focus** they ease apart one after another (30ms apart) and a card opens listing who did what and when, under the heading *Worked on this*.
- **Hidden** while the board is zoomed out or moving, unless the card is hovered or selected.
- It is a byline, not a control: no pen button on stickies, and never more than three faces.
- Under reduced motion the avatars and rows appear without moving.
