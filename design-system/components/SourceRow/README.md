One row of “Based on” inside a `Sheet`: the kind on its own line, the **full** title under it, and at most one pill at the right.

- Never clip the title. A shortened question is a different question.
- The pill shows evidence weight, or an **exception** (`changed`, `frozen`, `unavailable`, `archived`, `hidden`). The default link state, “Live”, is never shown.
- Rows have a `hover` fill instead of dividers.
